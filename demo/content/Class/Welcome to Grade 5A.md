# Welcome to Grade 5A

Hello, and welcome to our class folder!

Your teacher puts things here for you to use in class.

## What is in this folder

- **Worksheets**: practice sheets and a story to colour in.
- **Fractions practice.md**: maths questions about fractions.
- **Tortoise story questions.md**: questions about the Tortoise story.

## How to use it

1. Click a file to open it.
2. Read it, or print it if your teacher asks you to.
3. Do your own work in **My work**. You can find it in **All files**.

You can open the files in this folder, but you cannot change them.
That way they stay the same for everybody.

Have fun learning!
