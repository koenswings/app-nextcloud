# Lesson plan: halves and quarters

**Class:** Grade 5A  **Time:** 40 minutes  **Subject:** Maths

## Goal

Learners can show 1/2, 1/4 and 3/4 of a shape, and say which of two
fractions is bigger.

## You need

- The worksheet **Fractions practice.md** (in Grade 5A / Worksheets).
- The picture **Blank fraction circles.png**, printed or on screen.
- Paper circles or a round flatbread to cut, if you have them.

## Steps

1. **Start (5 min).** Fold a paper circle in half. Ask: "How many equal parts?
   What do we call one part?"
2. **Show (10 min).** Fold again to make quarters. Colour one quarter, then
   three quarters. Write 1/4 and 3/4 on the board.
3. **Practise (15 min).** Learners do Part 1 and Part 2 of the worksheet in
   pairs.
4. **Story problem (5 min).** Read Part 3 (sharing the bread) together.
5. **Check (5 min).** Ask three learners to show their answers on a circle.

## Using Nextcloud

- Learners open the worksheet from the **Grade 5A** folder.
- They can write their answers in a new file in **My work**.
- To see a learner's work, ask them to share the file with you.
