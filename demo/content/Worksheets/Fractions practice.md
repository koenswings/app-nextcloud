# Fractions practice

Name: ____________________

Use the picture **Blank fraction circles.png** to help you.
You can draw on a printed copy.

## Part 1: Colour the circle

1. Colour **1/2** (one half) of a circle.
2. Colour **1/4** (one quarter) of a circle.
3. Colour **3/4** (three quarters) of a circle.

## Part 2: Which is bigger?

Write the bigger fraction.

1. 1/2 or 1/4 → ______
2. 1/3 or 1/2 → ______
3. 2/4 or 1/4 → ______

## Part 3: Share the bread

Amara has one loaf of bread. She shares it equally with 3 friends.
There are 4 people in total.

1. What fraction of the loaf does each person get? ______
2. Draw the loaf and show the 4 equal parts.

## Challenge

Is **2/4** the same as **1/2**? Use a circle to show your answer.
