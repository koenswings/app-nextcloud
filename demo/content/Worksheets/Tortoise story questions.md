# Tortoise story questions

Name: ____________________

Read the story **How Tortoise got a crooked nose** (in this folder).
Then answer the questions. You can write or draw your answers.

1. What do Tortoise and Squirrel make to sell at the market?
2. Who is Tortoise's friend in the story?
3. What does Tortoise shout?
4. What happens to Tortoise's nose?
5. What could Tortoise do next time, instead of rushing in?
6. Colour in your favourite page of the story.

## Talk about it

With a partner, tell the story again in your own words.
What would you do if your friend needed help?
